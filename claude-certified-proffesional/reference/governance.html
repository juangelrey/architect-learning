Human-in-the-loop

  • Approval gate: irreversible or high-impact actions (money, deletion, external sends, posting). Graduated enforcement: auto-approve recoverable actions, escalate irreversible ones. OWASP
  • Escalation: low confidence, ambiguous or out-of-policy cases.
  • Sampling review: a % of auto outputs. Detective control that also feeds evals.
  • Regulated: Usage Policy in force (15 Sep 2025) high-risk domains: legal, healthcare, insurance, finance, employment and housing, academic testing/accreditation/admissions, media/journalism. A qualified professional reviews before finalising, and the person is told AI was used. The 12 Nov 2026 version adds credit, public benefits and healthcare access, and the reviewer must be able to change the recommendation. GDPR Art. 22: right to human intervention. AUP (in force); AUP (12 Nov 2026); Art. 22

Ethics

  • Bias eval: paired prompts that vary only demographics (Anthropic: 70 scenarios; prompting cut discrimination). Make it a release gate. Anthropic does "not endorse or permit" automated high-stakes decisions. Research
  • Transparency: chatbots must disclose AI at least at the start of each chat session (from 12 Nov 2026, the product interface also counts). High-risk outputs must disclose AI use. Cite quotes for explainability. AUP
