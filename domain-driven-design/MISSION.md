# Mission: Domain-Driven Design (interview-ready)

## Why
Prepare for an upcoming software architect / senior engineering interview where DDD may come up, both as concept questions and as live design exercises. Turn what I took from reading *Learning Domain-Driven Design* (Khononov) into fast, confident recall I can explain out loud.

## Success looks like
- Define any core DDD term from the book in one or two sentences, without notes
- Given a made-up business, name its subdomains and their types and justify each call
- Draw bounded contexts and a context map for a scenario, and justify each integration pattern
- Choose a business logic pattern, architecture, and testing strategy with Khononov's heuristics, and defend the trade-offs
- Explain how DDD relates to microservices, event-driven architecture, and data mesh

## Constraints
- Already finished the book; this is review and retention, not first exposure
- Give all four parts of the book equal weight
- Practice with made-up scenarios, not a real codebase
- Format: quizzes plus scenario exercises

## Out of scope
- Writing production code or framework-specific implementations
- DDD literature beyond what's needed to back up the book (Evans and Vernon only as supporting citations)
