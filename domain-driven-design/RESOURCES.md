# Domain-Driven Design Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _Learning Domain-Driven Design_ by Vlad Khononov (O'Reilly, 2021)](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/learning-domain-driven-design/9781098100124/)
  The primary text. Use for: everything. Cite chapters in lessons. The ch. 10 heuristics and the Appendix B exercise answers are high-yield for interviews.
- [Reference: _Domain-Driven Design Reference_ by Eric Evans (Domain Language)](https://www.domainlanguage.com/ddd/reference/)
  Evans's own concise definitions of every pattern. Use for: canonical wording of terms and context-map patterns.
- [Repo: DDD Crew, Context Mapping](https://github.com/ddd-crew/context-mapping)
  Community cheat sheet of context-map patterns. Use for: integration pattern definitions and visual notation.
- [Repo: DDD Crew, EventStorming Glossary & Cheat Sheet](https://github.com/ddd-crew/eventstorming-glossary-cheat-sheet)
  Sticky-note colors and grammar. Use for: EventStorming (ch. 12).
- [Article: Martin Fowler, "BoundedContext"](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/BoundedContext.html)
  Short explanation of bounded contexts and polysemous terms. Use for: ubiquitous language and bounded contexts.
- [Article: Martin Fowler, "What do you mean by 'Event-Driven'?"](https://martinfowler.com/articles/201701-event-driven.html)
  Origin of the event notification and event-carried state transfer terms. Use for: ch. 15.
- [Article: Martin Fowler, "CQRS"](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/CQRS.html) and ["Event Sourcing"](https://martinfowler.com/eaaDev/EventSourcing.html)
  Use for: chs. 7–8.
- [Article: Chris Richardson, "Transactional outbox"](https://microservices.io/patterns/data/transactional-outbox.html) and ["Saga"](https://microservices.io/patterns/data/saga.html)
  Use for: ch. 9 communication patterns.
- [Article: Zhamak Dehghani, "Data Mesh Principles and Logical Architecture"](https://martinfowler.com/articles/data-mesh-principles.html)
  Use for: ch. 16.
- [Article: Martin Fowler, "StranglerFigApplication"](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/StranglerFigApplication.html)
  Use for: ch. 13, brownfield modernization.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Virtual DDD](https://virtualddd.com/)
  Community-run online meetups and modeling sessions. Use for: practicing live modeling with practitioners, which is close to interview conditions.
- [DDD-CQRS-ES Discord / community](https://github.com/ddd-cqrs-es/community)
  Active practitioner chat. Use for: sanity-checking scenario answers and trade-off arguments.
